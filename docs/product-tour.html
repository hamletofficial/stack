Keep sustainability connected to the lease

Sustainability is part of the property assessment, not a substitute for commercial or legal due diligence.

What to check as a deal progresses

For a sustainable commercial property, review the energy-efficiency information and any stated Passivhaus or EnerPHit certification alongside the occupier’s requirements. Verify claims against appropriate documentation and confirm that evidence relates to the property being offered. Public portfolios display member-supplied information and do not independently certify buildings or validate claims.

As the deal moves from LOI to due diligence and contract, keep track of outstanding reviews, required documents, lease-option expiry dates and other relevant milestones. Use available reminders as prompts, while maintaining independent diary controls for contractual deadlines.

Good practice: only describe a property as certified or compliant where that claim is supported by current, relevant evidence. Hamlet helps connect workflow information; users remain responsible for checking source documents and contractual obligations.

One connected leasing workflow

Use Deals as the operational centre of the transaction. Your property resources and individual public portfolio present eligible listings; Contacts and Messages help coordinate the people and actions; the Dashboard gives a broader view; Archive preserves useful history; and your profile and settings support your day-to-day workspace.

The objective is simple: fewer disconnected records, clearer accountability and a more reliable path from sustainable property discovery to completed lease. Public portfolios show only the relevant member’s public listings; private property resources remain in the authenticated workspace.
